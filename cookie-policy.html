1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to improve their efficiency, and to provide information to the site owners. Similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, and tags may also be used; in this policy we refer to all of them collectively as "cookies."

This Cookie Policy explains how Fiama Luxury Club ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies on this website, and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

At Fiama Luxury Club, we use cookies to enhance your navigation, analyze internal statistics, and personalize your digital experience. We hold ourselves to the highest standards of data responsibility; your information is secure, strictly confidential, and is never sold, shared, or disclosed for unrelated purposes.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information without your consent, and we do not use them for intrusive advertising or profiling.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They include remembering your cookie preferences and enabling core functionality such as the contact form. They do not require consent.

Analytics & Statistics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website by collecting and reporting information anonymously (for example, which pages are visited and for how long). We use Google Analytics for this purpose. These cookies are only set after you give your consent.

Personalization Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make to provide a more personalized experience. They are only set after you give your consent.
