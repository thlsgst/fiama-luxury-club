The Destination

A private way to experience
Sardinia

Sardinia reveals a refined side of the Mediterranean. Clear, luminous waters, quiet coves, and coastal towns where elegance feels natural, never imposed. The island balances sophistication and authenticity with ease, offering a setting where time slows and experiences unfold organically.

From secluded beaches accessible only by sea to vibrant moments around exceptional food, wine and champagne, Sardinia invites a lifestyle centered on presence and pleasure. Days are shaped by light, movement and choice; evenings by connection and atmosphere.

With Fiama Luxury Club, Sardinia is experienced from its most privileged perspective: the water. Every detail is curated to feel effortless — from the route to the service, from gastronomy to ambiance — allowing guests to simply arrive, enjoy and belong.

Here, luxury is subtle, fluid and deeply personal. A space where everything flows, and nothing feels rushed.

Curated Itineraries

The Route

A Curated Route Through Sardinia

Handpicked destinations designed for those who value privacy, comfort, and seamless service.

Arcipelago Di La Maddalena

A protected marine area known for its crystal-clear waters, quiet anchorages, and preserved natural landscapes. Ideal for relaxed navigation and private stops surrounded by untouched scenery.

Isola Di Mortorio

Clear, calm waters and a rare sense of seclusion make Mortorio perfect for peaceful anchor stops and uninterrupted moments on board.

Cala Goffi & Cala Arancia

Two intimate coves offering sheltered waters and a calm atmosphere, ideal for swimming, sunbathing, and enjoying the coastline at a slower rhythm.

Spargi, Budelli, Razzoli & Caprera

A route defined by varied coastlines, open waters, and natural scenery that invites exploration at your own pace, with multiple stops shaped by the day's mood and conditions.

Porto Cervo, Porto Rotondo & Golfo Di Arzachena

Iconic marinas and coastal destinations that combine elegant infrastructure, renowned dining, and an active social scene, framed by Sardinia's distinctive shoreline.

The BAVARIA SR41's unique design and layout were developed in cooperation by Marco Casali from Too Design in Italy and the BAVARIA YACHTS development team. This collaboration produced unique and innovative ideas, including the iconic lounge sun area at the stern — a defining feature of the SR41.
